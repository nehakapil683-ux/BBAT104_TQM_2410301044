# BBAT104 TQM Viva Questions

## Restaurant Billing System – Q05: Reduce Response Time

### 1. What is the title of your project?

**Answer:**  
Restaurant Billing System – Q05: Reduce Response Time.

---

### 2. What is the quality goal assigned to your project?

**Answer:**  
The assigned quality goal is **Q05 – Reduce Response Time**.

---

### 3. Which technologies did you use?

**Answer:**  
Python, Tkinter/CustomTkinter, SQLite3, Pandas, Matplotlib, VS Code, and GitHub.

---

### 4. Why did you choose SQLite?

**Answer:**  
SQLite is lightweight, serverless, easy to integrate with Python, and suitable for a small desktop billing application.

---

### 5. What are the five Q05 features?

**Answer:**

1. Fast Search
2. Quick Navigation
3. Cached Dashboard
4. Database Indexing
5. Paginated Reports

---

### 6. What is Fast Search?

**Answer:**  
Fast Search improves record retrieval by using optimized search queries and database indexes.

---

### 7. What is Quick Navigation?

**Answer:**  
Quick Navigation allows users to move directly between important application sections such as Dashboard, Menu, Customers, Orders, and Reports.

---

### 8. What is Cached Dashboard?

**Answer:**  
Dashboard information can be stored temporarily in application memory so repeated dashboard access does not always require the same database calculations.

---

### 9. What is database indexing?

**Answer:**  
A database index is a data structure that can help the database locate records more efficiently for supported queries.

---

### 10. Why are indexes important for your project?

**Answer:**  
Indexes support faster retrieval of frequently searched or filtered data, such as menu items, customers, orders, and dates.

---

### 11. What is pagination?

**Answer:**  
Pagination divides a large report or dataset into smaller pages instead of displaying all records at once.

---

### 12. What is FMEA?

**Answer:**  
FMEA stands for **Failure Mode and Effects Analysis**. It is used to identify possible failures, their effects, causes, and risks.

---

### 13. What is RPN?

**Answer:**  
RPN stands for **Risk Priority Number**.

The formula is:

**RPN = Severity × Occurrence × Detection**

---

### 14. What is SIPOC?

**Answer:**  
SIPOC stands for:

- Supplier
- Input
- Process
- Output
- Customer

It provides a high-level view of a process.

---

### 15. What is a CTQ Tree?

**Answer:**  
CTQ means **Critical to Quality**. A CTQ Tree converts customer needs into measurable quality requirements.

---

### 16. What is a defect log?

**Answer:**  
A defect log records identified software problems along with their severity, status, causes, and corrective or preventive actions.

---

### 17. What is Pareto Analysis?

**Answer:**  
Pareto Analysis helps identify the categories contributing most to the recorded defects so improvement efforts can be prioritized.

---

### 18. What is a Fishbone Diagram?

**Answer:**  
A Fishbone or Ishikawa diagram is used to identify and organize possible root causes of a problem.

---

### 19. What is a Checksheet?

**Answer:**  
A checksheet is a structured form used to record observations, defects, or verification results systematically.

---

### 20. What is PDCA?

**Answer:**  
PDCA stands for:

- **Plan**
- **Do**
- **Check**
- **Act**

It is used for continuous improvement.

---

### 21. How does your project implement TQM?

**Answer:**  
The project applies TQM through customer-focused requirements, performance improvements, testing, defect analysis, FMEA, SQC techniques, and PDCA-based continuous improvement.

---

### 22. What is Poka-Yoke?

**Answer:**  
Poka-Yoke means mistake-proofing. In this project, input validation helps prevent invalid values such as incorrect quantities or prices.

---

### 23. How did you improve response time?

**Answer:**  
Response-time improvement was addressed through Fast Search, Quick Navigation, Cached Dashboard, Database Indexing, and Paginated Reports.

---

### 24. How did you verify the database?

**Answer:**  
The project includes database integrity verification for required tables and indexes.

---

### 25. How did you verify billing calculations?

**Answer:**  
Dedicated billing calculation tests verify expected totals for different item and quantity combinations.

---

### 26. How did you handle invalid input?

**Answer:**  
Input validation checks values such as quantity and price before they are accepted.

---

### 27. Why is testing important in TQM?

**Answer:**  
Testing provides evidence about software quality and helps identify defects that can be corrected through continuous improvement.

---

### 28. What is the role of GitHub?

**Answer:**  
GitHub is used for source-code management, version control, documentation, project tracking, and maintaining the project's commit history.

---

### 29. What is a meaningful commit?

**Answer:**  
A meaningful commit represents a specific project change, such as adding a feature, fixing a defect, adding documentation, or adding a test.

---

### 30. What would you improve in the future?

**Answer:**  
Possible future improvements include stronger automated testing, report export, additional performance monitoring, role-based access, and deployment improvements.

---
